Direcciones (Addresses)

2023  ·  14'22"  ·  HD / Super 8mm / 16mm

In Costa Rica there are no street addresses. A Costa Rican woman who has always felt a pressing anxiety by this lack of order tries to understand its origins — and discovers a larger story about human memory and place.
