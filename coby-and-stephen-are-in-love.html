Coby and Stephen Are In Love

2019, Single-channel HD Video, 30'41''

Produced and Directed by Carlo Nasisse and Luka Yuanyuan Yang | Featuring: Coby Yee and Stephen King | Edited by Maria Luisa Santos and Alex Winker | Cinematography by Carlo Nasisse | Sound Recorded by Luka Yuanyuan Yang | Colored by Alex Winker | Sound Design by Dan Rosato | Contributing Producers: Screening Room, Points North Institute, Jigsaw Productions | Film Consulting by Ruth Somalo | Supported by Tang Huanhuan, Rockbund Art Museum, Screening Room, Points North Institute, Jigsaw Productions

"Coby and Stephen Are in Love" is a love story between Coby, a 92-year-old retired nightclub dancer and icon from San Francisco Chinatown's golden age, and Stephen, a man 20 years her junior who has aspired to be an experimental filmmaker since the 1960s anti-war movement. They fell in love at a seniors dance, and like two opposite ends of a magnet, have been held together by an invisible force. They have created a glamorous world together through matching handmade outfits, dancing, and collage.
